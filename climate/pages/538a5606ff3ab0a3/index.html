2003 SEMCOG annual departure from median

Climate Office

2003 Annual departure from median

Forty-seven of 68 gauges (69 percent) were below their historical median. A-05 was 8.00 inches above median; W-42 was 9.14 inches below. Above-median areas generally lie south of a line from northwestern Livingston to southeastern Wayne, plus southeast Oakland and northeast Macomb. Most remaining areas were below median.

The map covers Livingston, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw and Wayne Counties. Crosses locate rain gauges; asterisks mark roof-exposure gauges. Black contour lines connect equal values.
