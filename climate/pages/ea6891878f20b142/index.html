2002 SEMCOG annual departure from median

Climate Office

2002 Annual departure from median

Almost all stations were below their long-term median; five were above it. A-05 was 2.55 inches above and M-03 was 0.65 inches above. O-07 was 11.95 inches below and O-28 was 11.94 inches below. Much of Livingston and Oakland Counties, southern Macomb, northern Wayne and northern Washtenaw were more than 4 inches below, with many stations more than 8 inches below.

The map covers Livingston, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw and Wayne Counties. Crosses locate rain gauges; asterisks mark roof-exposure gauges. Black contour lines connect equal values.
