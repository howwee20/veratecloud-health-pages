2003 Annual precipitation change from 2002

The region was generally wetter than 2002. A-02 increased 11.07 inches; L-05, 10.25; L-11, 9.95; and L-10, 9.93. A band from southwestern Livingston to central Oakland was over 6 inches wetter. Only six stations were drier; the largest decrease was 0.90 inches at M-03. Positive numbers mean more precipitation than 2002.

The map covers Livingston, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw and Wayne Counties. Crosses locate rain gauges; asterisks mark roof-exposure gauges. Black contour lines connect equal values.
