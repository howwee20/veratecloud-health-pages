2003 Calculated annual precipitation probabilities

The map gives the estimated fraction of years whose annual precipitation would be at or below the 2003 total, based on a gamma distribution and historical data through 2000. A-05 is highest at 0.95. Other above-median areas are generally 0.60–0.83. Low values of 0.03–0.15 occur in northeastern/east-central Wayne, southeastern Macomb and southwestern Oakland; two additional stations are 0.18. Values are dimensionless probabilities, not inches. Records as short as 16 years limit interpretation.

The map covers Livingston, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw and Wayne Counties. Crosses locate rain gauges; asterisks mark roof-exposure gauges. Black contour lines connect equal values.
