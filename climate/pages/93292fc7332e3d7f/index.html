2002 Calculated annual precipitation probabilities

The map gives the estimated fraction of years whose annual precipitation would be at or below the 2002 total, based on a gamma distribution and historical data through 2000. Most of the region is 0.30 or lower. A-05 is highest at 0.71; southeastern Livingston and southwestern Oakland include values below 0.005. A broad band from southeastern Livingston through northeastern Oakland, southern Macomb and nearby Wayne stations is below 0.05. Values are probabilities, not inches, despite the generic inch label in the source map key. Short records, some only 16 years, limit interpretation.

The map covers Livingston, Oakland, Macomb, Washtenaw and Wayne Counties. Crosses locate rain gauges; asterisks mark roof-exposure gauges. Black contour lines connect equal values.
