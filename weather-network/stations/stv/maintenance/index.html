04/10/2005: Data Quality Note

- It was determined that the clock on the Stephenson MAWN

station slowly drifted and was reporting incorrectly for

most of the winter. Because of the incorrect clock, the

observation times of the data reports are in error.

- The clock was corrected on 04/10/2005.

- Prior to the correction (Nov 2004 - April 09, 2005), the

observation times may be off by several hours. Eventually,

all data with incorrect time stamps will either be corrected

or removed from the MAWN database.
