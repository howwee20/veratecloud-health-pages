08/21/2009: Station Maintenance [1200]

- On 08/11/09 at 14:00, communication to/from the Chatham

datalogger was severed. Rather than install/bury new

short-haul modem cable, this opportunity was used to

upgrade the Chatham station to the CELL-IP Network. A

wireless broadband modem was installed before noon and

data-collection/monitoring is now controlled from the

EnviroWeather Office on the campus of Michigan State

University.

- MAWN Program - ABCF Loaded (60-minute power-cycle).

- Added Hourly Battery Voltage to MAWN Database tables

for Chatham.
