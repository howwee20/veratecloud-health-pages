04/13/2005: Station Maintenance: (sm)

- Replaced Temperature/Relative Humidity Sensor.

It was determined that the Temp/RH sensor at the West Olive

station has slowly drifted toward higher readings. The drift

can be traced back to August 2004. Air Temperature and

Relative Humidity readings between August 2004 - April 13, 2005

are not completely accurate and eventually may be removed from

the MAWN database.
