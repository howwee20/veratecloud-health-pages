07/19/2000: Station Update

- Comments: Difficult to get to. Access lane along

drainage ditch plowed over and planted with

corn. Station located in a corner of a corn

field next to drainage ditch, southwest of

dairy farm.

Station tripod not installed in the normal

position with leg facing south. This station

instead appears to be installed with leg

facing east. Tripod Legs are mounted to

wooden blocks, so position was left the same.

However, did unbolt during update so wind

sentry and solar panel could be installed

without the need of a ladder. Lag-bolt

on northwest facing leg snapped as it was

very rusted. Lag-bolts on other two posts

were replaced.

General condition: Complete overhaul as

the existing station showed much wear.

Concern about possible microclimate

variabilities due to corn planted near

the station and very tall weeds that

covered the entire station plot. Appears

that no care is taken to clean weeds and

grass at the station site.

Updated station using datalogger and hardware

that was used and displayed at AgExpo 2000.

Modem was installed and RJ11 phone wire was

connected as was the interface cable. There

was a green tab for Power and Ground, but

nothing was connected as the installation

was done as other modems (e.g. Belding). Dial

tone verified active phone line, but after

returning from field, learned that modem

wasn't picking up.
