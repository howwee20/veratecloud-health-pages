Review notes · October 13, 2026

Accessibility &
demo details.

What was tested, what the previews contain,
and where the demonstration ends.

Accessibility improvements

The weather previews retain the source sites’ branding and layout. Changes include keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and data tables, labeled forms, larger controls, responsive reflow, and text alternatives.

The station map opens a searchable chooser with 97 station links. The permanent directory contains 119 entries. Twenty-seven climate maps have expandable descriptions.

Recorded test results

  • The earlier 20-route build recorded official Silktide extension automated passes at WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • 40 desktop/mobile axe scans, plus six open-chooser scans, with no detected violations.
  • 93 browser tests passed after the October 13 refresh, covering keyboard access, reflow, reports, filters, and downloads. 128 parser/source checks and 99 regression unit checks also passed.
  • Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit checks, plus Chrome zoom at 200% and 400%.

The October 13 update refreshed weather data, corrected links and spacing, and added the office information page. This update was checked independently without rerunning Silktide. These are automated and browser-check results, not an MSU institutional scan or certification of full ADA/WCAG conformance.

Screen readers and content review

VoiceOver startup and speech were confirmed. A complete spoken-output test of all three weather workflows has not been recorded. Climate-map descriptions use approximate visual legend readings and require validation by the weather-data owner.

Demonstration scope

Included weather reports refresh hourly when MSU sources and the hosting scheduler are available. Each page shows its saved time and links to the current MSU source. Climate images and their matching descriptions retain the dated October 12, 2026 capture. Benzonia and SWMREC station and report pages are included. Other stations, some product reports, and custom requests open the original services; those destinations have not been remediated by this demonstration.

The EMR keeps its existing account sign-in and synthetic demonstration records. It is not intended for real patient care or real medical-record uploads.

Next stage

MSU’s review, complete assistive-technology acceptance, validated map descriptions, and integration of the remaining stations and services are required before any replacement of the official websites. The original MSU services have not been changed.
