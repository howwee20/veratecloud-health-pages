Accessibility &

What was tested, what the previews contain,
and where the demonstration ends.

Accessibility improvements

The weather previews retain the source sites’ branding and layout. Changes include keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and data tables, labeled forms, larger controls, responsive reflow, and text alternatives.

The station map opens a searchable chooser with 97 station links. The permanent directory contains 119 entries. Twenty-seven climate maps have expandable descriptions.

Recorded test results

  • 20 local routes with official Silktide extension automated passes at WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • 40 desktop/mobile axe scans, plus six open-chooser scans, with no detected violations.
  • 89 browser tests covering keyboard access, reflow, spacing, reports, filters, and downloads.
  • Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit checks, plus Chrome zoom at 200% and 400%.

The weather page files published here are unchanged from the tested build. These are automated and browser-check results, not an MSU institutional scan or certification of full ADA/WCAG conformance.

Screen readers and content review

VoiceOver startup and speech were confirmed. A complete spoken-output test of all three weather workflows has not been recorded. Climate-map descriptions use approximate visual legend readings and require validation by the weather-data owner.

Demonstration scope

The weather content is a saved October 12, 2026 snapshot. Benzonia and SWMREC station and report pages are included. Other stations, some product reports, and custom requests open the original services; those destinations have not been remediated by this demonstration.

The EMR keeps its existing account sign-in and synthetic demonstration records. It is not intended for real patient care or real medical-record uploads.

Next stage

MSU’s review, complete assistive-technology acceptance, validated map descriptions, and live data/service integration are required before any replacement of the official websites. The original MSU services have not been changed.
