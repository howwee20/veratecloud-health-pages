Accessibility and preview notes

This preview retains the three original site designs and refreshes the included public weather reports hourly when the source services are available. The saved time appears on each page; this is not a streaming feed. Climate maps retain their dated October 12, 2026 capture and matching text descriptions.

Included

The MAWN directory, Benzonia and SWMREC station pages and reports, the Agricultural Weather home, current and forecast indexes, and the Climate Office home and 27 climate maps.

Other stations, agricultural products and custom reports open their original services. Those services are outside this preview’s accessibility changes.

WCAG 2.2 AA target

The preview adds semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus, form labels, table headers, text alternatives, and layouts that reflow on small screens. The target follows MSU’s current technical guidelines. Automated checks are documented in the delivery notes.

This is not a certification of ADA or WCAG conformance. Screen-reader and assistive-technology review, provider review of the climate-map descriptions and access to the underlying gridded values, and review of the remaining original services are still required.

MSU uses Silktide and manual review. These demos have not received an MSU Silktide score or institutional sign-off.

Accessibility help

MSU Digital Accessibility provides guidance and a reporting service.
