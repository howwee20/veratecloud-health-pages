Accessibility and review notes

The three sites preserve their original branding, navigation, colors, and desktop layouts, with changes for keyboard access, readable tables, small screens, and text alternatives.

Available locally

All 119 station pages; 115 hourly reports, 115 daily reports and 119 maintenance logs; 100 recent raw station files; agricultural weather products and climate reference pages; and 27 current climate maps with local enlarged views.

Daily and hourly custom reports filter the available local dates and download CSVs. Eight reports for four retired stations are unavailable from the original source. Their pages say so explicitly.

Updates

Weather refreshes are scheduled hourly, subject to publisher and build availability. Climate maps update with matching dates, legends and regional text. Page timestamps identify saved source data; observation and forecast dates remain in the reports. This is not a streaming feed. Historical reference material is cached for up to 30 days.

WCAG 2.2 AA target

The site uses semantic headings, labelled controls, table headers, skip links, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operated station selection, responsive layouts, and text for missing observations. The target follows MSU’s technical guidelines.

This is an accessibility remediation preview for review, not an ADA certification or an institutional approval. Automated checks cannot establish full conformance. The newly expanded content has not received a new MSU Silktide score.

Scope and remaining review

  • Historical database queries, five-minute data and the original growing-degree-day query engine require a supported source-data integration.
  • Recent three-hour raw files are mirrored; other raw archive folders are inventoried only.
  • Regional climate-map text contains approximate sampled color bands. It does not reproduce every underlying grid value.
  • Imported historical graphics, PDFs and fixed-width text reports need content-specific accessibility review. Source text and available companion data are retained.
  • Two agricultural temperature graphics are denied by the source server; related daily maximum and minimum station observations are available locally.
  • Separate NOAA, Wisconsin, partner and institutional services remain clearly external destinations.

Production acceptance requires the site owners’ full-scope review, including manual assistive-technology checks, document/chart equivalents, and operational approval. MSU explains the limits of automated scans.
