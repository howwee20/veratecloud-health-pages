07/31/2002:  Rain Gage Repair: 
 
             - Funnel on the tipping-bucket rain gauge was  
               found to be partially plugged - collected  
               rainfall would pass through the funnel, but  
               at a very slow rate.  The obstruction was 
               cleared. 
 
             - Exact date/time the obstruction was introduced 
               is not known.  Care should be taken when using 
               the precipitation data from this station for 
               the months of June, 2002 and July, 2002.  The 
               total amount of precipitation (storm total)  
               should be fairly accurate, but there will be  
               an obvious lag in report times, especially with  
               the five-minute data, prior to when repairs were 
               made today.
