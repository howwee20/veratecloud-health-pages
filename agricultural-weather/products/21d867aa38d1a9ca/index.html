.DISCUSSION... 
Issued at 257 PM EDT Tue Oct 13 2026 
 
- Showers and isolated storms tonight 
 
A few showers and isolated thunderstorms have developed this 
evening in the far southern row of Michigan counties. This 
activity looks to sweep east and south of the area late this 
afternoon. The reasoning for this is the front is surging in the 
same direction and winds are becoming slightly divergent off of 
Lake Michigan.  
 
We expect additional precipitation to develop tonight however as 
the front drifts south into Northern Indiana. Mid level shortwaves 
working eastward through the Southern Great Lakes will interact 
with the nearby front and showers and a few storms will develop 
after sunset. The best chances for rain will come along the I-94 
corridor, but we will have chance pops all the way north into the 
I-96 corridor. Rainfall will be on the light side on the order of 
a tenth of an inch or less.  
 
- Showers and a few storms Thursday into Friday 
 
The most impactful weather of the forecast period will come 
Thursday into Friday as a fall system moves through the Great 
Lakes. The low will be deepening as it heads east, approaching 
990mb at it moves well east of the area. Winds will increase on  
the backside of the low on Friday with gusts over 40 mph certainly 
possible.  
 
The system will be deepening as shortwaves from both the northern 
and southern streams phase over the Great Lakes region. The result 
is the low swinging through along with deep moisture and lift. 
Rainfall will not be overly heavy with 0.25 to 0.75 inch amounts 
common. Some thunder is possible given the dynamic nature of the 
system, especially towards I-94 given surface dew points rising 
back in the lower 60s for a time.  
 
Lake effect rain showers are expected on Friday along with the 
driving northwest wind. Delta T's will push into the upper teens 
on Friday. It will be a wet day along the lakeshore for this 
reason.  
 
- Temperatures up and down through the 7 day forecast 
 
Temperatures will be on a bit of a roller coaster with 60s to 
around 70 for highs tomorrow. Highs will only reach the 50s on 
Friday and Saturday given the cold air aloft. We should push back 
towards 70 next Tuesday.

boundary. This boundary will be over southern Lower overnight and 
act as a focus for precipitation after 04z. Included a prob30 for 
showers, but it's possible there could be some thunder near the

.MARINE... 
Issued at 257 PM EDT Tue Oct 13 2026 
 
Waves continue to subside this afternoon and the Small Craft 
Advisory was allowed to expire on time at 200pm. The Whitehall 
spotter buoy is reporting a 2.4 foot wave at the present time, 
with South Haven at 1.6 feet. A downward trend should continue 
into tonight as high pressure builds in. There will be a increase 
in wave heights Wednesday into Wednesday evening as north winds 
funnel down the lakeshore in advance of the high. We feel these 
waves will likely stay below advisory criteria in the 2-4 foot 
range.  
 
The next time frame we are monitoring is Thursday night into 
Friday behind the passage of a fall low pressure system. Northwest 
wind crank up rapidly. We are likely looking at gale force winds 
with this system as cold air advection will be effective at  
building waves.
